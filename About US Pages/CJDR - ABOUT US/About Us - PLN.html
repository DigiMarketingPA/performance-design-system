About Performance Automotive Network

Performance Automotive Network is a family-owned automotive dealership group headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. With more than five decades of experience, we operate multiple franchise dealerships throughout Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky, along with additional franchise locations in Columbus, Ohio and the Greater Salt Lake City, Utah area.
